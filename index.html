1. Who is responsible

This policy explains how the WonderDoku app for Android (package ID de.mptech.wonderdoku) handles information. The developer and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is MP Tech DE, Berlin, Germany. You can reach us at marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com.

For the processing that Google carries out when it provides advertising, purchases and ratings, Google is responsible under its own privacy policy (see sections 3 to 6).

2. Data stored on your device

WonderDoku has no registration, no login and no user profile. To make the game work, it saves the following information only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • game progress: completed levels, stars earned, restored worlds, story scenes seen and tips already learned;
  • your Magic Stars balance, rewards, unlocked board themes and your VIP status;
  • settings: sound and music, language, automatic marks, reminder preferences and preferred reminder time;
  • small technical counters, for example whether the app has been opened before and when a rating request was last shown.

This information is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. We cannot see it, and it does not identify you.

Android device backup

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google, and we have no access to it. You can manage it in your Android settings (see Google's help page on backups).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

WonderDoku is free to play and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). Ads may appear as a banner on the home screen and as a full-screen ad between levels, after a level is completed. Ads are never shown while you are solving a puzzle. Rewarded video ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to earn Magic Stars.

WonderDoku is intended for teens and adults (13 and over). Its ad requests are set up as follows:

  • ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens (rating T or lower); ads rated for mature audiences are not requested;
  • from version 0.8.8, the app requests the Android advertising ID permission (AD_ID) for ads only (never for usage statistics). The advertising ID is used for personalized ads only where your choices allow it: with your consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland; unless you opt out in US states with privacy laws (section 13); and never after you delete it or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings;
  • in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are only requested once the consent status allows it, and Google uses your data for personalized ads only if you agree in the consent message (section 4); otherwise Google shows non-personalized or limited ads;
  • Google applies its own protections for younger users; for example, Google's policies do not allow personalized ads for users it knows to be under 18.

To deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical data from your device, such as the IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device type, operating system version, language, app information, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Google describes this in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • AdMob: ad content and audience settings

Ad auctions and bidding partners. Google AdMob chooses each ad in an auction. Besides Google's own advertising demand, ad buyers certified by Google and other ad exchanges that we may allow to bid through AdMob (for example Index Exchange, Magnite, OpenX, PubMatic or InMobi) can take part. Most of them work through Google's servers; the ad networks listed in the next paragraph also have their own software inside the app. To bid, they may receive information from the ad request, such as the IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device type, operating system, language, app information and your consent choices, and they process it under Google's rules and their own privacy policies. Their payments go through Google. Google lists the ad technology partners it works with, which also appear in the consent message where one is shown: Google AdMob: ad technology partners.

Ad networks with software in the app. From version 0.8.8, the app includes the software (SDK) of these ad networks, which bid for ads through Google AdMob and are paid through Google: AppLovin, Unity Ads and Liftoff. When the app requests an ad, they may collect device and app information (such as device type, operating system, language and app version), device identifiers including the advertising ID, the IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), interactions with ads and diagnostics, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. They receive your consent choices in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (they are listed in the consent message) and your US opt-out (section 13), and they process the data under their own privacy policies.

Buying VIP removes the home-screen banner and the ads between levels. Optional rewarded ads stay available for those who want them.

Legal basis (GDPR): personalized ads, and storing or reading information on your device where the law requires consent, are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25 of the German TDDDG), which the app asks for first (section 4). Showing non-personalized ads to fund a free app is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Consent choices

In regions where the law requires it, for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses the Google User Messaging Platform (a consent management tool certified by Google) to ask for and store your choices before ads are requested. Ads are only requested once the consent status allows it.

Where this applies, you can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and data → Privacy choices (called "Manage consent" before version 0.8.8). Your choice is stored on your device.

In US states with consumer privacy laws (for example California), the same entry in Settings lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising (section 13). This option is shown only where these laws apply.

The same choices also decide whether usage statistics and crash reports are collected (section 8). If you are below the age at which you can give this consent yourself in your country (between 13 and 16 in the EEA, depending on the country), please ask a parent or guardian before you make your choice, or choose not to consent.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy optional Magic Star packs and a one-time VIP upgrade. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We never receive your payment details, name or postal address.
  • Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation (product, purchase token and status) so that the app can deliver your item, confirm it to Google Play and restore VIP with "Restore purchases". This confirmation is handled on your device.
  • Google Play may provide us with order records (for example the product, the price and the country) for accounting, refunds and legal obligations.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations, for example tax records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Purchases are protected by the parental controls and purchase authentication that you can set up in Google Play.

6. Ratings and reviews

After you have restored some worlds, the app may occasionally show Google Play's official rating card (Google Play In-App Review). There is also a "Rate on Google Play" button in the settings. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive any personal data from the rating card, and we do not offer rewards for ratings.

7. Reminders

If you allow it, the app can show a gentle local reminder (at most once a day, never at night) when the magic wheel is ready or a puzzle is waiting. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No push server is used and no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the app settings or in Android's notification settings.

8. Analytics and crash reports

To understand how the game is played and to fix errors, WonderDoku uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere).

  • Usage statistics: game events such as a level started or completed, screens and story scenes viewed, the steps of a purchase (without payment details), the steps of an ad (requested, shown, reward received) and playing time while the app is open. Firebase uses a random identifier for this app installation. The app does not collect your name, e-mail address, the advertising ID or the Android ID, and ad storage and ad personalization stay turned off.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or a handled error occurs, technical information such as the error trace, device model, Android version, app version, the part of the game where it happened and a random installation identifier.

When it is collected: in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, only if you have given your consent in the consent message (storing and accessing information on your device, and measurement). If you do not consent, if you give only part of these choices, or if no consent could be requested yet (for example when the app was offline when you opened it), nothing is collected and crash reports kept on the device are deleted. Outside these regions, statistics and crash reports are collected as described here. Debug versions used for testing never send crash reports.

Legal basis: in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25 of the German TDDDG), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy and data → Manage consent. Elsewhere, where the law does not require consent, our interest in keeping the game stable and improving it.

Retention: Google keeps these data for a limited period under its retention policy and the settings of our project. A copy of the statistics may be stored in our Google Cloud project (BigQuery) for analysis.

If you have chosen in your Android settings to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may also give us aggregated statistics and crash reports about the app, which do not identify you.

9. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to use Google Play purchases and ratings.
  • Notifications: only for the optional reminders. On Android 13 and later, you are asked first.
  • Vibration: for short game feedback.

WonderDoku does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

10. Sharing and international transfers

We (MP Tech DE) do not receive your personal data, and we do not sell or rent it for money (for how US state laws define "selling" and "sharing", see section 13). The services in the app are provided by Google, as described in sections 3 to 8. For advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, these Google services may share limited data (such as the IP address, which can indicate an approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers, including the advertising ID from version 0.8.8, see section 3) with other companies: for ads, with the ad buyers and bidding partners that take part in Google's ad auctions and with the ad networks that have software in the app (section 3). This is what the Data safety section of WonderDoku's Google Play page shows as data that may be shared. Google and these partners may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the EEA, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on Standard Contractual Clauses (see Google's business data privacy information).

11. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • In the app, Settings → Privacy and data → Delete data on this device erases your progress, Magic Stars and settings. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling WonderDoku removes all of its data from your device. A device backup in your Google account is managed in your Android or Google account settings.
  • Your purchase history remains in your Google account, so VIP can be restored with "Restore purchases". Google keeps data according to its own retention policy.
  • If you write to us by e-mail, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it and to meet legal obligations.

12. Age requirement and children

WonderDoku is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no accounts, chat, social features or user-generated content, and it does not ask for names, e-mail addresses or photos. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens and the advertising ID is used for ads only, and for personalized ads only where your choices allow it (see section 3).

Parents or guardians who have questions, or who believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, can contact us at marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com. Because the app keeps game data only on the device, the data can also be removed in the app (section 11) or by uninstalling it.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access your personal data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Because WonderDoku stores game data only on your device and we hold no account data, we usually cannot identify you or find data about you. You can delete your data directly in the app (section 11). For processing by Google, you can use the tools in your Google Account or contact Google.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live, or the authority responsible for us: the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

US state privacy laws (for example California). We do not receive your personal data and we do not sell it for money. US state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA), define "selling" and "sharing" broadly. When you use WonderDoku in the United States, Google AdMob may use information collected through the ads in the app to show you ads based on your activity in other apps and websites (personalized or "targeted" ads), and the ad buyers in Google's auctions may receive limited information in order to bid (section 3). Unless you opt out, the app does not turn on Google's restricted data processing mode for these ad requests. Under these laws, this may count as "selling" personal information or "sharing" it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

  • Personal information involved: identifiers (such as the IP address and device identifiers), internet or other electronic network activity (such as interactions with the app and its ads) and the approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Recipients: Google, the ad buyers and bidding partners that take part in Google's ad auctions, and the ad networks with software in the app: AppLovin, Unity Ads and Liftoff (sections 3 and 10).
  • How to opt out: in the app, open Settings → Privacy and data → Privacy choices ("Manage consent" before version 0.8.8) and choose "Don't sell or share my data" (shown in US states with these laws). Your choice is stored on your device and passed to Google, which then restricts data processing for your ad requests, and to the ad networks with software in the app (section 3), so you are not shown personalized ads. You can also, on your Android device, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" (on older Android versions: "Opt out of Ads Personalization"); Google then no longer uses the advertising ID for personalized ads on that device.
  • Your requests: you can ask to know, correct or delete personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing, by e-mail (section 16). Because the game keeps its data on your device and we hold no identifiers that let us recognise you, we may not be able to link a request to specific data; you can delete the game data in the app (section 11), and Google offers its own tools in your Google Account. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
  • We do not have actual knowledge that we sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

14. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The Magic Stars balance is sealed against tampering. Communication with Google services is encrypted by those services.
