1. Who is responsible

This policy explains how the WonderDoku app for Android (package ID de.mptech.wonderdoku) handles information. The developer and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is MP Tech DE, Berlin, Germany. You can reach us at marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com.

For the processing that Google carries out when it provides advertising, purchases and ratings, Google is responsible under its own privacy policy (see sections 3 to 6).

2. Data stored on your device

WonderDoku has no registration, no login and no user profile. To make the game work, it saves the following information only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • game progress: completed levels, stars earned, restored worlds, story scenes seen and tips already learned;
  • your Magic Stars balance, rewards, unlocked board themes and your VIP status;
  • settings: sound and music, language, automatic marks, reminder preferences and preferred reminder time;
  • small technical counters, for example whether the app has been opened before and when a rating request was last shown.

This information is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. We cannot see it, and it does not identify you.

Android device backup

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google, and we have no access to it. You can manage it in your Android settings (see Google's help page on backups).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

WonderDoku is free to play and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). Ads may appear as a banner on the home screen and, from time to time, between levels. Rewarded video ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to earn Magic Stars.

Because WonderDoku is a game for families, its ad requests are configured for children:

  • every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment and as coming from a user under the age of consent;
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences (rating G) are requested;
  • with these settings, Google does not show personalized ads (interest-based ads or remarketing);
  • the app does not request the Android advertising ID permission (AD_ID), so the app does not provide the advertising ID for ads.

To deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical data from your device, such as the IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device type, operating system version, language, app information, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Google describes this in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • AdMob: child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings

Buying VIP removes the home-screen banner and the ads between levels. Optional rewarded ads stay available for those who want them.

Legal basis (GDPR): showing non-personalized ads to fund a free app is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where the law requires consent to store or read information on your device, the app asks for it first (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25 of the German TDDDG), see section 4.

4. Consent choices

In regions where the law requires it, for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses the Google User Messaging Platform (a consent management tool certified by Google) to ask for and store your choices before ads are requested. Ads are only requested once the consent status allows it.

Where this applies, you can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and data → Manage consent. Your choice is stored on your device.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy optional Magic Star packs and a one-time VIP upgrade. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We never receive your payment details, name or postal address.
  • Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation (product, purchase token and status) so that the app can deliver your item, confirm it to Google Play and restore VIP with "Restore purchases". This confirmation is handled on your device.
  • Google Play may provide us with order records (for example the product, the price and the country) for accounting, refunds and legal obligations.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations, for example tax records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Purchases are protected by the parental controls and purchase authentication that you can set up in Google Play.

6. Ratings and reviews

After you have restored some worlds, the app may occasionally show Google Play's official rating card (Google Play In-App Review). There is also a "Rate on Google Play" button in the settings. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive any personal data from the rating card, and we do not offer rewards for ratings.

7. Reminders

If you allow it, the app can show a gentle local reminder (at most once a day, never at night) when the magic wheel is ready or a puzzle is waiting. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No push server is used and no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the app settings or in Android's notification settings.

8. Analytics and crash reports

WonderDoku does not include Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics or any other third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. The app keeps a short list of game events (for example "level completed") in the device's memory for its own features. This list is not transmitted.

If you have chosen in your Android settings to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us aggregated statistics and crash reports about the app, which do not identify you.

9. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to use Google Play purchases and ratings.
  • Notifications: only for the optional reminders. On Android 13 and later, you are asked first.
  • Vibration: for short game feedback.

WonderDoku does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

10. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. The only third party involved is Google, as described in sections 3 to 6. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the EEA, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on Standard Contractual Clauses (see Google's business data privacy information).

11. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • In the app, Settings → Privacy and data → Delete data on this device erases your progress, Magic Stars and settings. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling WonderDoku removes all of its data from your device. A device backup in your Google account is managed in your Android or Google account settings.
  • Your purchase history remains in your Google account, so VIP can be restored with "Restore purchases". Google keeps data according to its own retention policy.
  • If you write to us by e-mail, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it and to meet legal obligations.

12. Children

WonderDoku is designed for families, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts, chat, social features or user-generated content, and it does not ask for names, e-mail addresses or photos. Ads are requested with child-directed treatment and limited to general-audience content (see section 3), in line with the Google Play Families Policy and laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

Parents or guardians who have questions, or who believe that a child's personal information has been shared with us, can contact us at marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access your personal data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Because WonderDoku stores game data only on your device and we hold no account data, we usually cannot identify you or find data about you. You can delete your data directly in the app (section 11). For processing by Google, you can use the tools in your Google Account or contact Google.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live, or the authority responsible for us: the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Residents of California and other places with similar laws: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

14. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The Magic Stars balance is sealed against tampering. Communication with Google services is encrypted by those services.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update at the top.
